About Blend

Blend is a producer and DJ with a deep passion for music. Through professional music education, she has developed the knowledge and expertise to shape her own distinctive sound.

Focusing on House and Afro House, both as a producer and behind the decks.

For Blend, music is about more than sound. It’s about creating emotion, connecting people and creating positive energy. Giving people emotions through music is one of the most beautiful things there is.
